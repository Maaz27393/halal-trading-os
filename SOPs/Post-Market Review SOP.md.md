# Post Market Review SOP

## Purpose

Review every completed trading day systematically.

The review is for:
- Trade documentation
- Rule compliance
- Execution quality
- Risk management
- Psychology
- Mistakes
- Lessons
- Improvements

The review must not silently modify Trading System rules.

## 1. Collect Today's Trades

For every completed trade, confirm the journal contains:

- Entry
- Exit
- Stop-Loss
- Target
- Quantity
- P&L
- R-Multiple
- Brokerage
- Taxes
- Net Profit
- Trade duration / holding period
- Relevant screenshots

## 2. Rule Compliance Review

Check each completed trade against:

- [[Market Filters]]
- [[Halal Rules]]
- [[Entry Rules]]
- [[Exit Rules]]
- [[Position Sizing]]
- [[Risk Management]]
- Applicable SOP

Classify each trade:

- Rule Compliant
- Rule Deviation
- Rule Violation

Do not classify a discretionary exit as a violation automatically.

## 3. Trade Quality Review

Evaluate:

- Setup quality
- Market context
- Entry quality
- Exit quality
- Risk management
- Execution quality

Record:

- Trade Quality Score: 1–5
- Execution Score: 1–5
- Risk Management Score: 1–5
- Rule Compliance Score: 1–5

## 4. Psychology Review

Record:

- Confidence: 1–5
- Fear: 1–5
- Greed: 1–5
- FOMO: Yes/No
- Revenge Trading: Yes/No
- Patience: 1–5
- Discipline: 1–5
- Followed Trading Plan: Yes/No
- Matrix Technique Check: Yes/No
- Decision Quality: 1–5
- Star Points Earned

The Matrix Technique question is:

> "Am I following my plan?"

## 5. Execution Errors

Record any applicable mistake:

- Late Entry
- Early Exit
- Ignored Stop Loss
- Wrong Position Size
- Ignored Confirmation
- Emotional Trade
- Rule Violation
- Overconfidence
- Chased +3–5% Stock
- Other

Add a short explanation where necessary.

## 6. Lessons Learned

Complete:

### What Worked

What was executed correctly?

### What Failed

What did not work or where did execution deviate?

### What Should Improve

What specific behaviour or process should improve?

### Would Take Again

Yes / No

## 7. AI Review

Claude may provide:

- Trade Quality Score
- Execution Score
- Risk Management Score
- Psychology Score
- Rule Compliance Score
- Improvement Suggestions

Claude must distinguish between:

- Verified rule violation
- Execution error
- Discretionary decision
- Research observation
- Potential improvement

Do not convert an observation into a rule automatically.

## 8. Research Identification

If a recurring pattern appears:

1. Record the observation.
2. Do not immediately change the Trading System.
3. Create or update the appropriate Research Item.
4. Define the hypothesis.
5. Collect sufficient evidence.
6. Pass the result through the Decision Gate.

Example:

Observation
→ Research Item
→ Evidence
→ Decision
→ Approved change, if justified
## 9. Daily Summary

At the end of the review record:

- Number of trades
- Wins
- Losses
- Win Rate
- Total P&L
- Total R
- Best trade
- Worst trade
- Rule violations
- Execution errors
- Key lesson
- Key improvement

## 10. No Silent Rule Changes

Post-market review must never directly modify:

- Entry Rules
- Exit Rules
- Risk Management
- Position Sizing
- Market Filters
- Halal Rules
- Trading System

Any proposed change must go through:
Research
→ Evidence
→ Decision Log
→ Explicit Approval
→ Trading System Update
→ Version Control
## Authority

This SOP governs the post-market review process.

It documents observations and evaluates completed trades. It does not create new Trading System rules.

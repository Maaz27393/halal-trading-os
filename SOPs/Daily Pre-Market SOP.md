# Daily Pre Market SOP

## 1. Style Selection

Select exactly one trading style the night before:

- Intraday
- BTST
- Swing

The style must not be decided or changed impulsively during the trading session.

## 2. Market Go / No-Go Check

Check the four approved market-breadth signals:

1. NSE Advance > Decline
2. India VIX < 20
3. Nifty 50 trend positive
4. Stock EMA20 > EMA50

### Scoring

- **4/4** → Strong Go
- **3/4** → Go if setup is strong
- **2/4** → Caution; exceptional setups only
- **0–1/4** → Avoid

## 3. Halal Universe

Confirm that all candidates belong to the current approved Halal Stocks universe.

Current documented count:

**174 stocks**

No fresh Shariah re-screen is required for a stock already on the approved list.

## 4. Nightly Scanner Pipeline

Run the approved nightly pipeline:

1. Institution Accumulation
2. 120-Day High
3. Volume Range Expansion
4. Promote qualifying candidates to Breakout Confirmation

Range Contraction candidates may be maintained on the secondary watchlist.

The retired two-scan workflow must not be used.

## 5. Watchlists

Maintain the approved watchlists:

- Halal Stocks
- Breakout Confirmation
- Secondary Breakout Confirmation

## 6. Candidate Preparation

For each candidate:

- Confirm Halal eligibility.
- Review market condition.
- Identify the selected trading style.
- Review relevant timeframe structure.
- Confirm the setup belongs to an approved strategy.
- Prepare the chart before the market opens.

## 7. Indicator Preparation

### Intraday

- EMA20/50
- VWAP
- AVP
- RSI-50
- RSI-10
- Pivot Points Standard

### BTST

- EMA20/50
- VWAP
- Volume
- Pivot Points Standard

### Swing

- EMA20/50
- FRVP
- RSI
- Pivot Points Standard

## 8. Risk Preparation

Before the session:

- Confirm available capital.
- Confirm maximum risk = 1% per trade.
- Confirm maximum 2 positions/day globally.
- Prepare position-sizing calculation.
- Confirm minimum R:R = 1:1.5.

## 9. Execution Windows

### Intraday

- 9:15–12:30 → Primary
- 12:30–1:30 → No-trade / A+ only
- 1:30–3:00 → Secondary
- 3:00+ → No fresh intraday entry

### BTST

- 3:10–3:25 PM only

### Swing

- No fixed intraday execution window documented.

## 10. Pre-Market Checklist

- [ ] Trading style selected night before
- [ ] Market Go/No-Go checked
- [ ] Halal universe confirmed
- [ ] Scanner pipeline completed
- [ ] Watchlists prepared
- [ ] Candidate charts prepared
- [ ] Risk limit confirmed
- [ ] Position limit confirmed
- [ ] Minimum R:R confirmed

## SOP Authority

This SOP documents the preparation process for the approved Trading System.

It must not create new trading rules.

Any proposed rule change must follow:

Research → Evidence → Decision Log → Trading System update.

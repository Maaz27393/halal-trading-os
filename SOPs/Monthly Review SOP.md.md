# Monthly Review SOP

## Purpose

Review the Trading OS over a full month to evaluate performance, process quality, recurring errors, research progress, and system integrity.

The monthly review does not automatically change active rules.

## 1. Monthly Performance

Review:

- Total trades
- Winning trades
- Losing trades
- Win Rate
- Total P&L
- Total R
- Average R
- Profit Factor, where available
- Maximum drawdown, where available
- Best trade
- Worst trade

## 2. Style Performance

Review separately:

- Intraday
- BTST
- Swing

Compare:

- Trade count
- Win Rate
- Total R
- Average R
- Rule adherence
- Execution quality
- Psychology

Do not change style allocation based on a single month's results.

## 3. Strategy Performance

Review each active strategy separately.

For each strategy record:

- Number of trades
- Win Rate
- Average R
- Total R
- Major losing patterns
- Major winning patterns
- Rule adherence

Historical backtests and live results must remain clearly separated.

## 4. Risk Management Review

Check:

- Maximum risk per trade
- Position sizing accuracy
- Maximum positions/day
- Stop-loss adherence
- Target adherence
- R:R compliance
- Any risk-limit violations

Any repeated risk problem must be investigated before considering a system change.

## 5. Rule-Adherence Review

Review recurring:

- Entry violations
- Exit violations
- Market-filter violations
- Halal-compliance issues
- Position-sizing errors
- Risk-management violations

Separate:

**System weakness** from **execution failure**.

Do not assume a repeated execution failure means the underlying rule is wrong.

## 6. Psychology Review

Review monthly patterns in:

- Confidence
- Fear
- Greed
- FOMO
- Revenge Trading
- Patience
- Discipline
- Decision Quality
- Matrix Technique usage

Identify recurring behavioural patterns that may require research or process improvement.

## 7. Execution Review

Identify recurring:

- Late entries
- Early exits
- Wrong position size
- Ignored confirmation
- Chasing
- Stop-loss errors
- Target errors
- Timing errors

Rank the most important recurring execution problems.

## 8. Research Review

Review the Research Pipeline:

- Ideas
- Hypotheses
- Active Experiments
- Backtests
- Validation
- Accepted
- Rejected
- Archived

For each active research item:

- Evidence status
- Current findings
- Decision required
- Next action

Research findings must not become Trading OS rules without the Decision process.

## 9. Decision Log Review

Review all decisions made during the month.

Confirm:

- Decision recorded
- Reason recorded
- Evidence recorded where applicable
- Affected document identified
- Version change recorded where required
- Decision status is clear

The Decision Log remains the final authority for confirmed decisions.

## 10. Trading OS Integrity Audit

Confirm:

- No undocumented rules were introduced.
- No approved rule was silently changed.
- Research remains separate from active rules.
- SOPs remain execution documents.
- Version numbers are consistent.
- Change Register is updated.
- Decision references remain connected.
- Claude project instructions remain aligned with the current system.

## 11. Data Quality Review

Check that the Trading Journal contains sufficient data for meaningful analysis.

Review missing:

- P&L
- R-Multiple
- Entry/Exit data
- Risk data
- Psychology fields
- Mistake categories
- Lessons
- Screenshots

Do not draw strong conclusions from incomplete data.

## 12. System Improvement Candidates

Classify observations as:

- Documentation improvement
- Process improvement
- Research required
- Experiment required
- No action

Do not directly convert an observation into a Trading System rule.

## 13. Decision Gate

A proposed Trading System change must follow:

Research
→ Evidence
→ Decision
→ Explicit Approval
→ Trading System Update
→ Version Control

If evidence is insufficient:

**No change.**

## Authority

This SOP governs the monthly review process.

It is an evaluation and governance layer.

It does not independently create or modify Trading System rules.

# Weekly Review SOP

## Purpose

Review the week's trading activity and identify recurring patterns without changing Trading System rules automatically.

## 1. Weekly Performance

Review:

- Total trades
- Winning trades
- Losing trades
- Win Rate
- Total P&L
- Total R
- Average R
- Best trade
- Worst trade
- Average trade quality
- Average execution score
- Average rule-compliance score
- Average psychology score

## 2. Style Performance

Review separately:

- Intraday
- BTST
- Swing

Compare:

- Number of trades
- Win Rate
- Total R
- Average R
- Rule adherence
- Execution quality
- Psychology

Do not conclude that one style is superior from a single week.

## 3. Rule Adherence

Review all deviations and violations.

Identify:

- Repeated rule violations
- Repeated execution errors
- Repeated psychological errors
- Recurring setup-quality problems

Separate:

**Rule problem** from **execution problem**.

Do not change a rule merely because it was difficult to follow.

## 4. Trade Quality

Review the highest- and lowest-quality trades.

Look for patterns in:

- Market condition
- Setup quality
- Entry timing
- Volume
- EMA structure
- VWAP / AVP / FRVP usage where applicable
- Exit behaviour
- Risk management

## 5. Psychology

Review:

- FOMO
- Fear
- Greed
- Revenge Trading
- Patience
- Discipline
- Confidence
- Decision Quality
- Matrix Technique usage

Identify recurring behavioural patterns.

## 6. Execution Errors

Group mistakes by category:

- Late Entry
- Early Exit
- Ignored Stop Loss
- Wrong Position Size
- Ignored Confirmation
- Emotional Trade
- Rule Violation
- Overconfidence
- Chased +3–5% Stock
- Other

Focus on repeated patterns rather than isolated incidents.

## 7. Lessons

Record:

### What Worked

...

### What Failed

...

### What Should Improve

...

### Most Important Lesson

...

## 8. Research Candidates

If a recurring pattern appears, classify it as:

- Observation
- Hypothesis
- Research Required

Do not turn weekly observations into Trading System rules.

Create/update a Research Item when sufficient recurring evidence justifies investigation.

## 9. Decision Review

Review decisions made during the week.

Check:

- New research decisions
- Approved changes
- Rejected changes
- Pending decisions
- Open Decision Gates

The Decision Log remains the authority for confirmed decisions.

## 10. Trading OS Integrity Check

Confirm that:

- No rule was changed without approval.
- No research finding was silently converted into a rule.
- No undocumented rule was introduced.
- Version control remains accurate.
- Decision records remain traceable.

## 11. Weekly Action Plan

Record only approved actions:

- Documentation updates
- Research items to investigate
- Experiments to run
- Behavioural improvements
- Process improvements

Do not create new trading rules from the action plan.

## 12. Weekly Conclusion

Summarize:

- Overall performance
- Strongest area
- Weakest area
- Most important recurring error
- Most important lesson
- Research requiring attention
- Actions for next week

## Authority

This SOP governs the weekly review process.

It is an analysis and feedback layer, not a rule-creation layer.

Any Trading System change must follow:

Research → Evidence → Decision Log → Explicit Approval → Trading System Update → Version Control.

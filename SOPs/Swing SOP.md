# Swing SOP

## 1. Style Selection

- Swing is selected the night before.
- Style is never decided intraday.

## 2. Swing Structure

- Swing analysis uses higher timeframes:
  - 1H
  - 4H
  - 1D
- Use Fixed Range Volume Profile (FRVP) for structure.
- Use a patience-based approach rather than forcing entries.

## 3. Entry Framework

The approved live execution chain is:

1. Stock passes the approved screening/scanner process.
2. Assess higher-timeframe trend and structure.
3. Use FRVP to identify relevant structure.
4. Wait for an approved pullback/breakout or momentum-continuation setup.
5. Confirm required confluence before entry.
6. Confirm R:R ≥ 1:1.5.
7. Confirm risk ≤ 1% of applicable capital.
8. Define SL and target before execution.

## 4. Indicators

Relevant active tools:

- FRVP — Swing structure
- EMA20/50 — trend filter
- RSI-50 — midline reference
- RSI-10 — momentum reference
- Pivot Points Standard — target-setting

## 5. Target

- Use Pivot Points Standard.
- Target = next relevant resistance level.
- Pivot Points Standard is the universal target-setting method across Intraday, BTST and Swing.

## 6. Risk Management

- Maximum risk = 1% of applicable capital per trade.
- Minimum R:R = 1:1.5.
- Maximum 2 positions per day globally across all styles.
- Cash-only.
- No leverage.
- No margin.
- No F&O.
- No short selling.

## 7. Halal Compliance

- Stock must be in the current approved Halal universe.
- Do not trade a non-Halal-screened stock.

## 8. Exit

- Stop-loss and target are defined before execution.
- Anchor SL/TP to the actual fill price.
- Matrix Technique applies to behavioral decisions:

> "Am I following my plan?"

If **No** → exit immediately and stop trading for the day.

## 9. Important Strategy Status

Pure EMA pullback logic was confirmed unsuitable for short swing holding periods of approximately 7–25 days.

The system therefore requires a stronger compression/breakout or momentum-continuation framework rather than treating the old EMA pullback approach as the complete Swing strategy.

## 10. Research Boundaries

The following are **not currently active Swing rules**:

- GANN levels
- ATR Pullback
- RSI Ignition
- Standalone Volume Breakout

These remain research/shelved items unless formally adopted through the Decision process.

## 11. Post-Trade

Record the trade in the Trading Journal and complete the post-trade review.

## Authority

This SOP documents execution of approved Trading System rules.

It must not create or independently modify Trading System rules.

Any proposed change follows:

Research → Evidence → Decision Log → Trading System update.

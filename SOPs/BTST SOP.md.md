# BTST SOP

## 1. Style Selection

- BTST is selected the night before.
- Style is never decided intraday.

## 2. Candidate Selection

Use the approved Halal universe and curated scanner pipeline.

Priority:

- Strong stock structure
- Breakout setup
- Volume confirmation
- VWAP confirmation

## 3. Entry Window

BTST entries are permitted only:

**3:10 PM – 3:25 PM**

No BTST entry outside this window.

## 4. Entry Conditions

Required sequence:

1. Day-high breakout.
2. Retest of breakout area.
3. Volume confirmation.
4. VWAP confirmation.
5. Confirm acceptable R:R ≥ 1:1.5.
6. Confirm risk ≤ 1% of applicable capital.
7. Enter using the approved position-sizing calculation.

## 5. Avoid Late / Extended Candidates

Skip BTST candidates that are already approximately **3–5% up intraday**.

The purpose is to avoid late-entry / trap risk.

## 6. Stop-Loss and Target

Before entry:

- Define stop-loss.
- Define target.
- Anchor SL/TP to actual fill price.
- Target using Pivot Points Standard → next resistance.
- Minimum R:R = 1:1.5.

## 7. Position Limits

- Maximum 2 positions per day globally across all styles.
- Maximum risk = 1% of applicable capital per trade.
- Cash-only.
- No leverage.
- No margin.
- No F&O.
- No short selling.

## 8. Next-Day Exit

### Gap-Up Open

- Book partial profit quickly according to the approved BTST exit procedure.

### Flat / Weak Open

- Exit immediately rather than allowing the position to deteriorate.

## 9. Halal Compliance

- Stock must be in the current approved Halal universe.
- No trading outside the approved universe.

## 10. Post-Trade

Record the trade in the Trading Journal and complete the post-trade review.

## 11. Authority

This SOP documents execution of approved BTST rules.

It must not create new Trading System rules.

Any proposed change follows:

Research → Evidence → Decision Log → Trading System update.

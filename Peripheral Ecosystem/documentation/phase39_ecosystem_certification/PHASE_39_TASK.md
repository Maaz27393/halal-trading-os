# Phase 39 — Full Ecosystem Integration & Certification Master Specification

## Objective
Establish a comprehensive system-wide integration and certification gate validating that Phases 1–38 operate together as one coherent, secure, and rigorously bounded ecosystem, producing a unified machine-readable certification result.

## Core Tracks & Blueprints
- **39A_DEPENDENCY_CONTRACT_VALIDATION.md** — Cross-phase dependency and contract integrity audits (Phases 1–38).
- **39B_END_TO_END_EVIDENCE_FLOW.md** — Evidence lineage tracking from raw data through intelligence to investigation and notification.
- **39C_FAILURE_INJECTION_MATRIX.md** — Deliberate simulation and containment verification for system failures.
- **39D_INTELLIGENCE_CONSISTENCY.md** — Epistemic boundary verification ensuring intelligence conclusions maintain correct status (`OBSERVED`, `PROJECTED`, `HYPOTHETICAL`, `CANDIDATE_CAUSE`).
- **39E_GOVERNANCE_BOUNDARY_AUDIT.md** — Strict audit of non-execution boundaries and read-only invariants.
- **39F_UNIFIED_CERTIFICATION.md** — Master certification orchestrator producing `CERTIFIED`, `CERTIFIED_WITH_WARNINGS`, or `FAILED`.

## Critical Boundaries
- **Analysis Only & Read-Only:** Zero automated remediation, zero auto-scaling, zero automated decisions, zero execution authority.

# Phase 27 — Knowledge & Decision Intelligence Layer Master Specification

## Objective
Establish a provider-neutral, evidence-oriented retrieval and decision intelligence layer that indexes and cross-references operational events, data quality states, regression reports, audit logs, and analytical feeds from Phases 20–26.

## Core Tracks & Blueprints
- **27A_KNOWLEDGE_CONTRACTS.md** — Standardization of operational events and evidence into retrievable knowledge objects.
- **27B_KNOWLEDGE_INDEXING.md** — Indexing mechanism capturing outputs from regression runners, control centers, and audit logs.
- **27C_CROSS_DOMAIN_RETRIEVAL.md** — Unified search and filtering across operational, quality, and governance domains.
- **27D_DECISION_CONTEXT.md** — Structuring retrieved evidence into coherent decision context packages for investigation.
- **27E_VALIDATION.md** — Verification testing ensuring provenance preservation, zero fabricated evidence, and read-only safety.

## Critical Boundaries
- **Evidence-Oriented & Read-Only:** Strictly provides explanatory context and investigation support; zero execution authority or trading capability.
- **No Unauthorized Writes:** Maintains immutable retrieval indexes without altering underlying source data.

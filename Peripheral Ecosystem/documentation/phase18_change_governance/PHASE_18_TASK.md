# Phase 18 — Continuous Validation & Change Governance Master Specification

## Objective
Establish a formal baseline freeze and rigorous change-governance framework to protect the certified Halal Trading OS ecosystem from regression, contract drift, and unauthorized boundary violations during future development cycles.

## Core Tracks & Blueprints
- **18A_CERTIFIED_BASELINE_FREEZE.md** — Definition and immutable reference of the Phase 17 certified baseline.
- **18B_CHANGE_IMPACT_FRAMEWORK.md** — Classification matrix for evaluating new domains, contracts, and operational updates.
- **18C_AUTOMATED_REGRESSION_GATE.md** — Pipeline requirements for testing future modifications before integration.
- **18D_VERSIONED_ARCHITECTURE_CONTRACTS.md** — Strict semantic versioning for canonical contracts and adapters.
- **18E_CONTROLLED_CHANGE_LOG.md** — Authoritative tracking template for logging ecosystem changes, risk analysis, and rollback plans.

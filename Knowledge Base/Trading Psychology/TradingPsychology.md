#   Trading Psychology

## 1. What It Is

**Trading Psychology** is the study of a trader's mental state, emotional framework, and cognitive discipline during market analysis and trade execution.

## 2. How It Works

Successful trading goes against natural human instincts. Evolutionary cognitive biases designed to keep humans safe (such as loss aversion or status quo retention) trigger emotional reactions like fear, greed, revenge trading, and hesitation. Mastering trading psychology requires building strict operational routines, accepting risk, and treating trading as a systematic business.

## 4. Major Cognitive Biases & Mitigation

**The Emotional Cycle of Uncontrolled Trading:** Hope → Euphoria (Over-leverage) → Anxiety (Market Pullback) → Panic (Dumping at Bottom) → Depression → Revenge Trading

|Cognitive Bias|Psychological Trigger|Direct Impact on Trading|Mitigation Protocol|
|---|---|---|---|
|Loss Aversion|Pain of a loss feels 2× more intense than the joy of a gain|Hesitation to enter trades; refusing to exit losing trades.|Enforce physical stop losses; treat losses as standard business costs.|
|Endowment Effect|Overvaluing items simply because you own them|Holding failing stocks too long believing "they are special."|Adhere strictly to dynamic trend exit criteria.|
|Anchoring Bias|Fixing decisions on irrelevant past reference prices|Expecting a stock to return to its previous all-time high.|Base analysis solely on current price action context.|
|Confirmation Bias|Seeking information that agrees with existing positions|Ignoring bearish warning signals on open long positions.|Actively seek out counter-theses before executing trades.|

## 5. Strengths

- **Decisive Execution Advantage:** Mindset and emotional control represent over 80% of long-term trading success.
- **Consistency Enforcer:** Eliminates impulse trades, revenge trading, and fear-based exit decisions.
- **Prevents Trader Burnout:** Establishing physical and mental routines maintains long-term focus and clarity.

## 6. Weaknesses

- **Difficult to Quantify:** Psychological discipline cannot be coded or calculated as easily as technical indicators.
- **Requires Constant Self-Awareness:** Deep-seated emotional habits take months or years of active discipline to reprogram.
- **Vulnerable to Personal Stress:** Outside life stress directly impacts market discipline and execution quality.

## 7. Best Practices

- **Build a Daily Routine:** Execute a morning routine balancing spiritual, mental, and physical health before market open.
- **Journal Every Trade:** Log emotional states during entry, management, and exit to identify psychological patterns.
- **Accept Risk Before Entry:** Mentally accept that the risk amount is gone the second the trade is executed.

## 8. Common Mistakes

- **Revenge Trading:** Immediately re-entering the market with larger position sizing after a loss to "get money back."
- **FOMO (Fear Of Missing Out):** Chasing extended prices after a breakout has already occurred.
- **Blaming the Market:** Refusing personal accountability for bad trades and blaming brokers, news, or algorithms.

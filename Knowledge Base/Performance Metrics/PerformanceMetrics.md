#   Performance Metrics

## 1. What It Is

**Performance Metrics** are quantifiable measurements used to track, evaluate, and optimize a trader's efficiency, risk exposure, and portfolio consistency.

## 2. How It Works

Performance metrics track equity curves beyond total percentage gains. By evaluating metrics like the **Profit Quadrant**, **Risk-to-Reward Ratio (R:R)**, and **Maximum Drawdown**, traders isolate systemic execution flaws (such as holding big losses) and optimize profit retention.

## 3. Formulas

1. **Risk-to-Reward Ratio (R:R):** $$R:R = \frac{|Target\ Price - Entry\ Price|}{|Entry\ Price - Stop\ Loss\ Price|}$$
    
2. **Maximum Drawdown (MDD):** $$MDD = \frac{Peak\ Value - Trough\ Value}{Peak\ Value} \times 100$$
    
3. **Sharpe Ratio:** $$Sharpe\ Ratio = \frac{R_p - R_f}{\sigma_p}$$
    

## 4. The Profit Quadrant & Metric Benchmarks

**The Profit Quadrant:**

||Small Loss|Big Loss|
|---|---|---|
|**Small Profit**|Ideal / Average (Base business model)|Avoid at all costs (Destroys expectancy)|
|**Big Profit**|Ideal (Target state — maximized asymmetric R)|Ideal / Volatile (High risk exposure)|

|Metric Parameter|Minimum Acceptable Rule|Professional Standard|Purpose / Value|
|---|---|---|---|
|Risk-to-Reward Ratio|Minimum 1 : 1.5|1 : 2.0 or 1 : 3.0|Ensures target reward outweighs capital risked.|
|Max Loss Per Trade|Capped at 1% of Capital|0.5% – 1% of Capital|Protects capital base against drawdowns.|
|Profit Quadrant Target|Zero Big Losses|Focus on Small Losses & Big Profits|Eliminates account-killing outlier losses.|

## 5. Strengths

- **Pinpoints Execution Flaws:** Reveals whether poor performance stems from low R:R, position sizing, or bad entry timing.
- **Protects Equity Curves:** Tracking drawdowns alerts traders to market regime shifts before severe capital destruction occurs.
- **Drives Business Optimization:** Converts trading from gambling into a measurable, scalable performance operation.

## 6. Weaknesses

- **Can Cause Over-Analysis:** Excessively monitoring real-time performance metrics can induce execution hesitation during drawdown periods.
- **Metrics Require Trade Volume:** Statistical validity demands a continuous, well-logged trade journal over time.
- **Backward-Looking:** Reflects historical execution performance, not guaranteed future trade outcomes.

## 7. Best Practices

- **Eliminate Big Losses:** Focus strictly on avoiding the "Big Loss" section of the Profit Quadrant.
- **Enforce Minimum 1:1.5 R:R:** Reject any setup where potential reward is less than 1.5 times the stop-loss distance.
- **Review Metrics Periodically:** Analyze trade performance monthly and quarterly to refine entry and exit execution rules.

## 8. Common Mistakes

- **Accepting Low Risk-Reward Trades:** Entering setups with 1:0.5 R:R, requiring an unrealistically high win rate to stay profitable.
- **Ignoring Drawdowns:** Continuing to scale position sizes while actively sitting in a deep portfolio drawdown.
- **Failing to Keep a Trade Journal:** Attempting to optimize performance without tracking exact execution metrics.

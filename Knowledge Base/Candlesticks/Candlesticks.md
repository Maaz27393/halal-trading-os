#  Candlestick Analysis

## 1. What It Is

A **Candlestick** is a visual representation of price movement over a defined period, displaying four core data points: Open, High, Low, and Close (OHLC).

## 2. How It Works

Candlesticks construct a body between the Open and Close prices, while upper and lower wicks (shadows) mark the absolute High and Low reached during the period. Green (or white) bodies represent bullish sessions (Close > Open), while red (or black) bodies represent bearish sessions (Close < Open).

## 3. Formulas

$$Real\ Body\ Size = |Close - Open|$$ $$Total\ Candle\ Range = High - Low$$ $$Body\ Ratio = \frac{Real\ Body\ Size}{Total\ Candle\ Range}$$

## 4. Key Patterns & Mechanics

**Bullish Patterns:** Hammer (small body, long lower wick, forms at downtrend bottom) · Bullish Engulfing · Piercing Line (>50% penetration) · Three White Soldiers

**Bearish Patterns:** Shooting Star (small body, long upper wick, forms at uptrend peak) · Bearish Engulfing · Dark Cloud Cover (<50% penetration) · Three Black Crows

|Pattern Name|Type|Candle Count|Context / Description|
|---|---|---|---|
|Hammer / Inverted Hammer|Bullish Reversal|Single|Small body with long lower wick; forms at downtrend bottom.|
|Shooting Star / Hanging Man|Bearish Reversal|Single|Small body with long upper wick; forms at uptrend peak.|
|Bullish / Bearish Engulfing|Reversal|Double|Second candle body completely covers previous candle body.|
|Morning Star / Evening Star|Reversal|Triple|Large candle, small star body, followed by large reversal candle.|
|Three Soldiers / Crows|Continuation / Strong Reversal|Triple|Three consecutive long-bodied candles closing near session highs/lows.|

## 5. Strengths

- **High Visual Clarity:** Quickly reveals market momentum, price rejection, and volatility.
- **Early Warning Capability:** Reversal patterns like Hammers or Engulfing candles provide early entry signals.
- **Universal Standard:** Applicable across all trading platforms and liquid assets worldwide.

## 6. Weaknesses

- **Noise on Lower Timeframes:** Single-candle signals on 1-minute or 5-minute charts have high false-signal rates.
- **Lack of Price Target:** Candlesticks signal potential directional pivots but do not give target calculations.
- **Context Dependent:** A candlestick pattern is useless without surrounding structural context.

## 7. Best Practices

- **Context Is King:** Always evaluate candlestick patterns at key Support and Resistance levels.
- **Trade What You See:** Trade current confirmed candle structures, not predictions of how unclosed candles might look.
- **Wait for Close:** Never execute a trade on an unclosed candle; wait for the session boundary to finalize.

## 8. Common Mistakes

- **Memorizing Without Understanding:** Learning pattern names without grasping the underlying battle between supply and demand.
- **Trading Mid-Range Candles:** Executing reversal trades on candles that form in the middle of a sideways consolidation range.
- **Ignoring Overall Trend:** Buying bullish hammer setups in a dominant higher-timeframe downtrend.

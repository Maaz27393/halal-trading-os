#   Market Structure

## 1. What It Is

**Market Structure** is the framework that categorizes price movement into organized trends, ranges, and transitional phases by analyzing directional price swings (Highs and Lows).

## 2. How It Works

Markets cycle through distinct structural states: Uptrends (Higher Highs & Higher Lows), Downtrends (Lower Highs & Lower Lows), and Consolidation (Equal Highs & Equal Lows). Institutional order flow changes cause a **Break of Structure (BOS)** or **Change of Character (CHOCH)**, signaling trend continuation or structural reversals.

## 3. Formulas

1. **Higher High (HH):** $$High_t > High_{previous\ swing}$$
    
2. **Higher Low (HL):** $$Low_t > Low_{previous\ swing}$$
    
3. **Break of Structure (BOS):** $$Close_t > Swing\ High_{prior}\quad (Bullish\ BOS)$$
    

## 4. Structural States & Breakout Verification

**Uptrend Structure:** A sequence of rising swing points where each Higher High (HH1 → HH2 → HH3) is separated by a Higher Low (HL1 → HL2) that stays above the prior low.

**Downtrend Structure:** A mirrored sequence of falling swing points where each Lower High (LH1 → LH2) is followed by a Lower Low (LL1 → LL2 → LL3) that stays below the prior low.

|Real vs. Fake Breakout Factor|Real Breakout Sign|Fake Breakout Sign (Fakeout)|
|---|---|---|
|Candle Close|Strong candle body close beyond key level|Only a wick extension; body closes inside range.|
|Volume|Significant volume expansion|Low or declining volume.|
|Retest Behavior|Retest holds broken level as new S/R|Immediate price collapse back into consolidation.|
|Trend Alignment|Breakout moves in direction of main trend|Breakout pushes against dominant higher-timeframe trend.|

## 5. Strengths

- **Clear Directional Bias:** Establishes whether the market is bullish, bearish, or neutral.
- **High-Probability Pivot Points:** Identifies precise structural levels for setting stop losses and targets.
- **Filters Counter-Trend Trades:** Prevents traders from fighting the dominant market direction.

## 6. Weaknesses

- **Subjective Swing Definition:** Traders may disagree on what constitutes a major vs. minor swing high/low.
- **Lagging Pivots:** Market structure shifts are only confirmed after swing points are fully formed.
- **Complex Multi-Timeframe Alignment:** Lower timeframes often conflict with higher timeframe structural trends.

## 7. Best Practices

- **Trade with Higher Timeframe Bias:** Align short-term entries with main weekly or daily chart structures.
- **Wait for Body Close:** Confirm a Break of Structure only when a candle body closes beyond the previous swing high/low.
- **Watch S/R Flips:** Expect broken structural resistance to act as new support on pullbacks.

## 8. Common Mistakes

- **Confusing Wicks with BOS:** Treating a temporary wick sweep past a high as a confirmed trend continuation.
- **Ignoring Consolidation Ranges:** Trying to trade trend-following setups inside a sideways channel.
- **Chasing Extended Moves:** Entering long positions after price has already printed multiple Higher Highs without pulling back.

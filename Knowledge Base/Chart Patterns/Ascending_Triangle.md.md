
#### Definition

An Ascending Triangle is a bullish chart pattern characterized by a horizontal upper resistance line connecting equal price peaks and an ascending lower support line connecting higher price lows. It represents a period of price consolidation where buyers are aggressively pushing prices up while sellers maintain a static ceiling.

#### Structure

- **Upper Boundary:** A horizontal resistance level tested at least twice.
    
- **Lower Boundary:** An upward-sloping trendline connecting higher swing lows.
    
- **Apex:** The point where the upper resistance and lower support converge.
    

#### Market Psychology

Sellers continue to offer supply at a fixed price level (horizontal resistance). However, with every pullback, buyers step in at progressively higher prices, demonstrating increasing aggressive demand and willingness to absorb supply until sellers are overwhelmed.

#### Entry

- **Aggressive Entry:** Enter on the candle close beyond the upper horizontal resistance line.
    
- **Conservative Entry:** Enter on a price pullback/retest of the broken horizontal resistance line now acting as support.
    

#### Stop Loss

Placed just below the most recent higher swing low within the triangle formation, or below the breakout candle's low.

#### Target

Calculated using the "Measured Move" technique: Measure the vertical distance at the widest part of the triangle (the base) and project that distance upward from the breakout point.

#### Volume Behaviour

Volume contracts progressively during the formation of the triangle and surges significantly above average on the breakout candle, confirming buyer absorption.

#### Confirmation

A decisive candle body close above horizontal resistance accompanied by a spike in Relative Volume.

#### Invalidation

A breakdown below the rising support trendline or a close back inside the triangle after a breakout attempt (Fake Breakout).

#### Strengths

- Provides precise risk-defined parameters.
    
- High success rate when aligned with the dominant higher-timeframe uptrend.
    

#### Weaknesses

- Susceptible to premature fake breakouts in choppy/sideways macro environments.
    

#### Common Mistakes

- Buying before the upper resistance is cleanly broken.
    
- Ignoring low volume on the breakout, which often leads to a false breakout.
    

#### Best Timeframe

1-Hour, 4-Hour, and Daily charts.

#### Works Best In

Established uptrends during trend continuation phases.

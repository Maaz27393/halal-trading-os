# Price Action Analysis

## 1. What It Is

**Price Action** is the study of raw price movement over time without primary reliance on lagging technical indicators. It relies on analyzing candlestick shapes, wicks, support/resistance levels, and order flow to understand market context.

## 2. How It Works

Price action reflects the real-time supply and demand dynamic between buyers and sellers. By reading candle bodies, wicks, and structural highs/lows, traders evaluate whether buyers (demand) or sellers (supply) are in control at critical price levels.

## 3. Formulas

1. **Spread Calculation:** $$Spread = Ask\ Price - Bid\ Price$$
    
2. **Wick Rejection Ratio:** $$Upper\ Wick\ Ratio = \frac{High - \max(Open, Close)}{High - Low}$$ $$Lower\ Wick\ Ratio = \frac{\min(Open, Close) - Low}{High - Low}$$
    

## 4. Key Attributes & Mechanics

**Wick Rejection Analysis:** An upper wick rejection at the high of a move signals strong selling pressure (sellers pushed price back down). A lower wick rejection at the low of a move signals strong buying pressure (buyers pushed price back up).

|Order Flow Element|Concept|Market Meaning|
|---|---|---|
|**Bid Price**|Highest price a buyer is willing to pay|Represents instantaneous demand floor.|
|**Ask Price**|Lowest price a seller is willing to accept|Represents instantaneous supply ceiling.|
|**Upper Wick**|Price spike rejected by sellers|Supply dominance; selling pressure.|
|**Lower Wick**|Price spike rejected by buyers|Demand dominance; buying pressure.|

## 5. Strengths

- **Zero Indicator Lag:** Provides immediate market signals directly from tick and candle data.
- **Clean Charts:** Eliminates indicator noise and clutter, simplifying trade execution.
- **High Adaptability:** Works effectively in trending, ranging, and highly volatile environments.

## 6. Weaknesses

- **High Discretion:** Requires extensive practice to interpret context correctly without objective mathematical indicator lines.
- **Emotional Bias Vulnerability:** Subjective interpretation can lead traders to see non-existent setups due to confirmation bias.
- **Requires Deep Context:** A single price candle means nothing without structural context.

## 7. Best Practices

- **Trade the "Story" Over Names:** Focus on the underlying story of rejection and control told by wicks and bodies rather than memorizing pattern names.
- **Focus on Key Levels:** Only execute price action setups (pin bars, engulfing bars) when they occur at key Support & Resistance zones.
- **Combine with Volume:** Validate price action rejection bars with above-average volume.

## 8. Common Mistakes

- **Trading Isolated Candles:** Taking a hammer candle trade in the middle of a tight consolidation range without a supporting level.
- **Ignoring Spread:** Trading illiquid stocks where a wide Bid-Ask spread eats into position profitability.
- **Chasing Parabolic Moves:** Buying large green candles after price has already moved far from support.

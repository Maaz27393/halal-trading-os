#  Comprehensive Trading Glossary

## 1. What It Is

The **Trading Glossary** is an alphabetical reference dictionary defining key market terminology, technical slang, order execution jargon, and regulatory/Shariah framework concepts.

## 2. Market Terminology & Definitions

### A–C

- **Anchoring Effect:** Cognitive bias where decisions are tied to irrelevant historical reference prices.
- **Ask Price:** The lowest price a seller is willing to accept for a security.
- **Average True Range (ATR):** A volatility indicator measuring the average absolute price range over a specified period.
- **Bearish / Bear:** Market sentiment expecting price to decline.
- **Bid Price:** The highest price a buyer is willing to pay for a share.
- **Black Swan Event:** A rare, highly unexpected event causing extreme market volatility.
- **Bollinger Bands:** A volatility indicator using standard deviation bands around a central moving average.
- **Bonus Shares:** Free additional shares distributed to existing shareholders based on equity holdings.
- **Break of Structure (BOS):** Price breaking past a prior swing high or swing low, confirming trend continuation.
- **BTST (Buy Today, Sell Tomorrow):** A short-term trading technique where positions are held overnight and closed the next day.
- **Bullish / Bull:** Market sentiment expecting price to increase.

### D–H

- **DEMAT Account:** An electronic account used to hold financial securities electronically.
- **Dividends:** Corporate earnings returned directly to shareholders as cash or stock payouts.
- **Double Top / Bottom:** M-shaped or W-shaped reversal patterns marking trend exhaustion.
- **Endowment Effect:** A cognitive bias causing traders to overvalue assets simply because they own them.
- **Exponential Moving Average (EMA):** A weighted moving average that places greater significance on recent price data.
- **FII / DII:** Foreign Institutional Investors and Domestic Institutional Investors (large funds driving market volume).
- **Front Running:** Illegal trade execution by an insider ahead of a large, known customer order, to profit from the expected price movement.
- **Golden Cross:** Bullish moving average crossover (e.g., short EMA crossing above long EMA).

### I–P

- **Intraday Trading:** Buying and selling financial instruments within the same trading session.
- **Limit Order:** An order to buy or sell a security at a specific price or better.
- **Loss Aversion:** The psychological tendency where the pain of losing capital is felt twice as intensely as the pleasure of gaining it.
- **Market Capitalization:** Total market value of a company's outstanding shares.
- **Market Order:** An order requesting immediate execution at current prevailing market prices.
- **Moving Average Convergence Divergence (MACD):** A trend-following momentum oscillator tracking the distance between two EMAs.
- **Position Sizing:** Calculating the precise number of shares to buy to keep trade risk within predefined limits.
- **Pump and Dump:** Illegal market manipulation artificially hyping an asset price before dumping holdings onto retail buyers.

### R–Z

- **Relative Strength Index (RSI):** A bounded momentum oscillator measuring the velocity of recent price changes on a scale of 0 to 100.
- **Resistance:** A price ceiling where supply overwhelms demand, halting upward moves.
- **Risk-to-Reward Ratio (R:R):** The mathematical ratio comparing trade risk to expected profit payout.
- **Scalping:** Trading style focused on capturing small profits over seconds or minutes.
- **Shariah Compliance:** Adherence to Islamic financial law (avoiding Riba/interest, Gharar/excessive ambiguity, and Maysir/gambling).
- **Stock Split:** Corporate action increasing outstanding share count while proportionally reducing share price to enhance liquidity.
- **Stop Loss:** A predefined exit order designed to cap potential loss on a trade.
- **Support:** A price floor where demand overwhelms supply, halting downward moves.
- **Swing Trading:** Holding trade positions across multiple days to weeks to capture swing moves.
- **Trailing Stop:** A stop loss order that dynamically adjusts as price moves in favor of the trade.
- **VWAP (Volume Weighted Average Price):** Intraday benchmark calculating the average price weighted by volume.
- **WhipSaw:** A high-volatility false breakout move that triggers stop losses in both directions.

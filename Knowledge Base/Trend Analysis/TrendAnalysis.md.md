#  Trend Analysis

## 1. What It Is

**Trend Analysis** is the process of determining the dominant price trajectory (direction) of a financial asset over a given timeframe.

## 2. How It Works

Trends reflect sustained buyer or seller dominance over time. Trend analysis breaks price trajectory into three primary categories: Uptrend, Downtrend, and Sideways/Ranging Market. To build a reliable trade thesis, traders utilize **Multi-Timeframe Trend Structure**, aligning short-term trade entries with long-term primary trends.

## 3. Formulas

1. **Trendline Angle Slope (θ):** $$Slope\ m = \frac{Price_2 - Price_1}{Time_2 - Time_1}$$
    
2. **Moving Average Alignment Filter:** $$Uptrend\ Regime: Price > EMA_{20} > EMA_{50} > EMA_{200}$$
    

## 4. Trend Types & Multi-Timeframe Alignment

**Trend Types:** Uptrend (sequence of Higher Highs/Higher Lows) · Downtrend (sequence of Lower Highs/Lower Lows) · Sideways Trend (Ranging, roughly Equal Highs & Lows)

|Trend Hierarchy Level|Primary Timeframes|Operational Role / Purpose|
|---|---|---|
|Main Trend (Macro)|Weekly / Daily Charts|Determines macro directional bias (only buy in Main Uptrends).|
|Mid Trend (Intermediate)|Daily / 4-Hour / 1-Hour Charts|Identifies major counter-trend pullbacks or consolidation setups.|
|Small Trend (Micro)|15-Min / 5-Min Charts|Pinpoints precise entry timing, stop placement, and trigger execution.|

## 5. Strengths

- **Aligns Trader with "Path of Least Resistance":** Significantly increases trade win rates by trading in the direction of major momentum.
- **Filters Out Low-Probability Trades:** Eliminates counter-trend trades that suffer from low R:R and quick reversals.
- **Simplifies Execution Rules:** Provides absolute clarity (e.g., "only look for longs above the daily 200 EMA").

## 6. Weaknesses

- **Trend Transitions Cause Losses:** Trend-following systems experience drawdowns when a market shifts from trending to ranging.
- **Lagging Confirmation:** Waiting for full trend confirmation can mean missing early reversal moves.
- **Timeframe Alignment Conflicts:** Small trends can show a strong downtrend while the Main trend remains strongly bullish.

## 7. Best Practices

- **Follow the Multi-Timeframe Rule:** Ensure short-term trade direction matches higher timeframe main trend direction.
- **Trade Channels:** Draw parallel trendlines to enclose price action and identify channel bounce setups.
- **Use Moving Averages as Trend Filters:** Use macro averages (like 50 EMA or 200 EMA) to establish bias before analyzing setups.

## 8. Common Mistakes

- **Picking Tops and Bottoms:** Attempting to short strong uptrends or buy crashing downtrends without structural confirmation.
- **Ignoring Main Trend Context:** Taking intraday short signals when weekly/daily charts are printing strong higher highs.
- **Over-Fitting Trendlines:** Forcing trendlines through arbitrary wicks to justify a trade bias.

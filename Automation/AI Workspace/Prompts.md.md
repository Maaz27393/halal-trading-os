# Trading OS Prompts

## Purpose

Central repository for reusable prompts used with Claude and other AI tools for the Halal Trading OS.

## Core Prompts

### Trading Analysis

Use for structured analysis of trades, setups, market conditions, and execution.

### Post-Trade Review

Use for reviewing completed trades against the applicable strategy, SOP, risk rules, and execution requirements.

### Research

Use for investigating hypotheses, experiments, and potential Trading OS improvements.

### Decision Analysis

Use for evaluating research findings before a formal decision is recorded.

### System Review

Use for reviewing Trading OS documentation for consistency, duplication, missing links, or undocumented changes.

## Prompt Rules

- Prompts must not instruct AI to invent Trading OS rules.
- AI must use existing Obsidian documentation as the knowledge base.
- AI must check [[Claude Instructions]] before performing system-level work.
- AI recommendations do not become Trading OS rules automatically.
- Formal decisions belong in [[Decision Log]].
- Preserve evidence classification.
- Prefer existing notes, templates, folders, and dashboards.
- Do not create duplicate documentation.

# AI Inbox

Temporary capture area for AI-generated ideas, observations, research leads, and items requiring review.

## Rules

- Do not treat Inbox items as approved Trading OS rules.
- Classify each item before moving it elsewhere.
- Move valid research ideas to [[Research]].
- Move formal decisions to [[Decision Log]].
- Move approved Trading OS changes to the appropriate Trading System document.
- Delete or archive items that are rejected or no longer useful.
- Do not leave important decisions permanently in Inbox.

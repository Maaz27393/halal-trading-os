# Halal Rules

## Halal Screening

Any stock under consideration must belong to the approved Halal Stocks universe.

### Stage 1 — Fundamental Screening

Screen using Screener.in:

- Market Cap > ₹2,000 Cr
- Price > ₹500
- ROE > 11%
- Current Ratio > 1
- Debt/Equity < 0.5
- Quick Ratio > 1

Re-screen quarterly.

### Stage 2 — Shariah Screening

Use the approved Shariah screening process:

- Zoya
- Islamicly
- Musaffa or equivalent approved source

### Hard Exclusions

Do not trade companies involved in:

- Alcohol
- Tobacco
- Conventional insurance
- Conventional interest-bearing finance

## Structural Trading Constraints

- Long-only
- Cash-only
- No leverage
- No margin
- No F&O
- No short selling

## Approved Halal Universe

Current documented count:

**174 stocks**

The Halal universe is re-screened every 3–4 months.

The count is rolling and may change at each screening cycle.

## Daily Verification

For a stock already on the approved 174-stock watchlist:

- No additional daily Halal verification is required.

Fresh two-stage verification is required only for:

1. A newly added stock not yet on the approved list.
2. The scheduled 3–4 month watchlist revision.

## Authority

Halal compliance is non-negotiable.

Do not change these rules based on an individual trade.

Any proposed change must follow:

Research → Evidence → Decision Log → Trading System update.
